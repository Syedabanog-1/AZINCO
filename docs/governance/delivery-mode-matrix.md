## Modes [Source §3]

| Mode | Meaning | What AZINCO may hand to the client |
|---|---|---|
| A — Prepare and File | AZINCO prepares and files with the authority on the client's behalf | Filing receipts — **only** where licensed and authorised |
| B — Prepare and Hand Over | AZINCO prepares; the client (or their adviser) files | Reviewed working papers and return drafts |
| C — Partner-Reviewed | A licensed partner in the country reviews and signs | Partner-signed deliverables |
| D — Bookkeeping Only | Books and management reports, no tax filing | Ledgers, reconciliations, management reports |
